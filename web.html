eswy, 2025

Web Designer

ESWY is an interactive web experience designed as an extension of the music video, transforming its concept of seven lives into a playable digital experience. I designed and developed the game around the idea of moving through these seven lives, translating the narrative and visual language of the music video into an interactive format. The project explored how a music video’s world could continue beyond the screen and become something the audience could actively experience.

libra

Web App

LibraLog is a personal life-tracking web experience built around the idea of equilibrium — that life is a constant balancing of different forces, emotions, experiences, and moments. The app allows users to log how different aspects of their life are going and observe how those elements shift over time. Rather than treating life as a linear measure of progress, LibraLog presents it as an ever-changing balance, where periods of growth, uncertainty, joy, and difficulty all contribute to the larger picture. I designed and developed the experience as an exploration of how something as intangible as “how life is going” can be translated into an interactive digital system.
