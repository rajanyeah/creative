fayeda, 2026

Jugaad Motion Pictures

Creative Director & Writer

Fayeda is a music video produced with Jugaad Motion Pictures, built around the idea of a traditional Punjabi swayamvar set in an akhada. I developed and wrote the entire creative concept in collaboration with singers Rudraaksh and Krish Kapoor, and served as the Creative Director for the project. Drawing from Punjabi visual culture, the video was designed around the energy, colour, and character of an akhada, with the set and visual environment developed to support the story. I also handled the complete set design, shaping the physical world in which the swayamvar unfolds.

Performer: Rudraaksh

heer ban jana, 2026

Jugaad Motion pictures

Director's Assistant

Heer Ban Jana is a music video produced with Jugaad Motion Pictures, built around the story of a lover boy who secretly edits himself into his ex-girlfriend's wedding video. The concept plays with heartbreak, humour, and the absurdity of holding on to someone through the one thing he knows best — editing. Featuring singer Bharat, the video was directed with a strong focus on visual storytelling and framing. I worked as the Director's Assistant, where I was responsible for handling shot framing and supporting the execution of the visual direction throughout the shoot.

Performer: Bharat
Youtube: heer ban jana

strapped, 2026

Nick Mendoza

Main Lead

Strapped is a short film centred around a young man who finds himself trapped by his girlfriend and is forced to figure out a way out. I played the lead role, carrying the character through the film's tension, confusion, and escalating desperation. The project gave me the opportunity to explore a performance driven less by dialogue and more by physicality, reactions, and the character's attempt to outsmart his situation.

Director: Nick Mendoza
